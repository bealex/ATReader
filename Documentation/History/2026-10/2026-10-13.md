# 13 October 2026

## A picture of the page, for the launch to open on

An app closed on a book came back on it, but through a moment of bare paper while the chapter was
set. The reader now keeps a picture of its sheets as the app stops being in front, and the next
launch shows it until the page is drawn.

Recorded on the simulator with the kept picture flipped upside down, so the picture and the page
could be told apart frame by frame:

| System at launch | Picture kept in | What the launch shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Light | Paper | The picture from the first frame, then the page faded in over it |
| Dark | Paper | Dark paper, then the page. No picture |
| Dark | Night | The picture from the first frame, then the page |
| Light | Night | Light paper, then the page. No picture |

Three things went wrong on the way.

- **A picture read from a file is one point to a pixel.** It came back three times the window's size
  and matched nothing. The window's size is kept beside it, and the scale is worked out from the two.
- **The picture is taken as the app resigns, and not in the background.** The system turns the app to
  the other appearance there to photograph it.
- **The first frame knows no scene.** Asking `SystemAppearance` for the theme answered nothing on the
  first pass, and "Restoring your session" showed for a frame before the picture did. The root's own
  colour scheme is right from the start, since no book has held the window to a page's yet.

The picture fades in with the system's own crossfade out of the launch screen, which isn't the
app's to change.

Not measured on a phone, where setting the page takes longer and the picture stands for more frames.
