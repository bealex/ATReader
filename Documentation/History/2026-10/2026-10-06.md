# 2026-10-06

## Two windows on one library

The app has one window today, and the Info.plist doesn't ask for more. An audit of what a second
one would break found six things, all fixed.

- **A stale reading place could win.** Every open reader writes its place when the app leaves the
  screen. With one book open in two windows, the one left idle wrote last as often as not. A reader
  now writes only after it has moved.
- **Removing a book rewrote the whole library** from that window's own list, which would drop a book
  another window had just added. It deletes the one book now.
- **New chapters could be counted twice.** Two sweeps could both ask which chapters were new before
  either stored the contents, and the badge counts add up. Asking and storing are one call now.
- **The bundled first book could be offered twice,** since its mark went down after the reading-in
  and every window runs the offer. The launch housekeeping ran once a window for the same reason.
- **A window didn't hear about another's changes.** Marking a book read, putting one away, merging
  and reordering wrote to the store and told nobody. They raise the inbox's signal now, with the
  sender named so its own window doesn't redraw twice.
- **The orientation lock nudged the first scene only,** and two reports read the first scene's
  window.

Not tried on a device with two windows open: the simulator here can't be driven by hand, and the
app doesn't ask for multiple scenes. Two tests cover the store's half.

## The iPhone Duo simulator

Xcode 27.1 has no Simulator app. DeviceHub replaces it, and the fold is a slider there. `simctl` and
`devicectl` can read the hinge angle (`devicectl device motion hinge-angle`) and can't set it. The
device has two built-in screens, `--display=1` for the cover and `--display=3` for the inner one, and
`simctl io` records one of them at a time.
