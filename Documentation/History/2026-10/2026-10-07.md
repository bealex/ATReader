## Ink from one theme on paper from another

Coming back to an open book after the system had gone from light to dark, or the other way, showed
the new paper under the old ink for about a second.

The paper was a view's background, set the moment the theme changed. The ink is an attribute of the
typeset lines, so it changes only when the chapter is set again, off the main actor. While the app
is suspended the system wakes it to say the appearance changed, then suspends it again, so the
setting was cut off half way and finished only once the reader was back and looking at it.

Now the page keeps the theme its words are in until the new sheet is on screen, and the reader asks
for background time to finish the setting. `ReaderSettings` also reads the system's style when it's
made, so the first page of a launch isn't set light and then again dark.

The fault didn't reproduce on the simulator. Brought back from the background after a change of
appearance, the build without the fix was already right by the first frame sampled, about a third of
a second in. Why wasn't established; a Mac sets a chapter faster than a phone, and the simulator may
not suspend the app as a phone does. With the fix, frames sampled through the same change, and
through one made with the book in front, never showed ink and paper from different themes.
