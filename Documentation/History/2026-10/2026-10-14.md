# 14 October 2026

## The progress bar slid in with the controls

Revealing the controls turns the page number into "page/length" with a bar beside it, through a
crossfade. The bar slid in from the left and its fill grew instead of simply fading in.

Both moves came from laying the caption out inside the transition's animation block. The bar's frame
was animated from wherever it last stood, and its fill is a bare `CALayer`, which animates a frame
change of its own accord when its view lays out. The caption now sets every frame and lays out with
animations and layer actions off, inside the block, so the transition only crossfades. A recording on
the simulator shows the bar at its full width from the first frame of the fade.

## Note markers drawn as subscripts

A note's marker came out below the line, as a subscript. So did every `<sup>` figure, and `<sub>`
figures stood above the line.

The offsets were signed for `CTLineDraw`, where the flipped text matrix made a positive
`.baselineOffset` sink and the markers took a negative one. On 7 October the page went to drawing each
run with `CTRunDraw`, so a run could take its share of the theme's ink, and there the same matrix
takes a positive offset upwards. The signs are flipped in `NoteMarker` and `ScriptMarker`.

The test that should have caught it asserted only that the offset was negative. It's replaced by two
that draw a page and compare the bottom row of the figure's ink with the bottom row of the words
before it.
