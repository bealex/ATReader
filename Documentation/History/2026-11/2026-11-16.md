## The top margin after a partial fold

Folding the phone part way and opening it again left the page with a top margin 62 points deeper. A
log of what the reader took from its window showed why. A partial fold moves the app to the folded
screen for a moment, where the system bar runs across the top and the window's top inset is 82 points,
for the camera. Back on the open screen the bar returns down the side, but the window goes on reporting
that 82-point top for as long as the hinge stays part way open, and only drops it once the hinge opens
fully.

In every state with the bar down a side the real top inset is nought, since the column holds the
status and the camera. The page now ignores the window's top inset whenever the bar stands there.
