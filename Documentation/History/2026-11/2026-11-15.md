## Where the folding phone's status pill ends

On a folding phone the system stands its own bar down one side, and the time and signal sit in a pill
at the top of that column. The reader's close button goes below the pill, and nothing reports how far
down it reaches. The status bar's frame comes back two points tall, or zero once the reader is up; the
safe area covers the whole column; and the vertical bar API gives only its edge.

So the depth comes from screenshots of the device. Folded and upright, the pill runs from about 75 to
157 points down, under the camera; unfolded and on its side, from about 31 to 95. The close button
stood at 120 at first, where the folded pill covered it, then at 184, far too low unfolded. It now
stands at 184 on a screen narrower than 560 points and at 120 on a wider one, with room either way for
one more status item.
