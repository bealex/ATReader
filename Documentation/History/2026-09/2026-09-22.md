# 2026-09-22

## A page with four lines of room left at its foot

Reported mid-chapter: 23 lines, and 84pt of the 760pt measure empty under them. Measured off the
screenshot the pitch is 29.6pt, which is the setting's 26.6pt line plus the leading pegged at its 3pt
cap, so the lines were set at the setting in force and the page simply had too few of them.

Cut again from the report's own chapter at the report's own setting, the same first line takes 29
lines and fills the measure to the point, forwards and from behind alike. The same text comes to 23
lines in any measure from 600pt to 670pt. The window less its bands twice over is 664pt, which is the
trap the reader's page is built to avoid: a size read from the layout instead of the window. Which
path did it this time isn't known, and the reader didn't change a setting.

Two things came of it. A sheet that settles with a page a line or more short mid-chapter is cut again,
since a page cut against the measure in force never is. And the debug report writes `layout.txt`: the
measure each page on screen was cut against beside the one the reader asks for, the window and sheet,
and the last sixty cuts, so a page like this next time says where its measure came from.
