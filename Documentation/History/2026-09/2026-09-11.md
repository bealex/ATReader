# 2026-09-11

## Planks, ink and writers' names

Light mode's planks were the card colour and read as bright bars across a dark bookcase. They're #A4A4AB
now, and the series names and lines on them are darker to match. Dark mode is unchanged.

A writer filed under two spellings, with and without a patronymic or with the family name first, had two
cards. `WriterNames` files every spelling in the library under one: the words sorted, ё read as е, and a
patronymic dropped from a three-word name. Two different patronymics stay apart, and a bare given name
joins nobody. The spelling a card shows is the one most books carry.

## Unfinished parts hidden, Profile regrouped

Top and the catalogue half of Search are hidden behind `-at-show-catalogue` until they're worked on, and
the design catalogue moved from its own tab to a row in Profile, in Debug builds only. Profile is four
groups: Author.Today, with the last check for updates under the nickname; other sources; Backup, with its
own buttons and a confirmation before anything is restored or forgotten; and Other.

## Parallel UI tests

Tried and dropped. Four clones took 947 s and two took 979 s, against 497 s run one at a time. Under the
load the reader tests timed out, and the unit tests collided in the shared directories local books are
kept in. The catalogue tests stay split into four classes, which costs nothing.

## Covers and spines

A real cover no longer carries the volume plate, which stays on spines and on volumes the reader doesn't
hold. A spine's title keeps as much room from the top as the plate keeps from the bottom.

## Series titles

`Fixtures/Titles/`, which git ignores, lists the 538 books in the backup that belong to a series, and
`SeriesTitlesTests` checks the shelf against its last column. Reading it gave the
rules now in `SeriesNumbering.title`: a volume word goes where its figure is the file's own volume and
becomes "/N" otherwise, a colon after the series' name keeps the name, and a title that was only the
series' name and index is called by the name. 62 titles changed, every one by those rules.

## Co-written books

A book with several writers stands on each of their cards. A series written together throughout goes to
every card whole. Otherwise the shared books stand alone on the co-author's card, because a run holding
only some of a series' volumes would show the rest as gaps.

## One writer a card

A series put together out of several writers' runs had a card of its own headed by all of them. It now
stands whole on each writer's card, so no card names more than one. A co-author only gets the book on a
card they already have: the backup holds an anthology of fifteen writers, and giving each a card for it
would have added a dozen one-book cards.

A series titled "Name: Subtitle", "Name 2: Subtitle", "Name 3: …" kept the name on its first book alone.
Where the series' other titles number the name, the colon no longer keeps it. `SeriesTitlesTests` had
been building each series without reading its numbering, which the shelf does, so it never saw this; it
reads it now. `AT_TITLES_SHELF` writes `shelf-titles.tsv`, the whole library's series books as the cards
show them, for going through by eye.

## A book's series by hand

One LitRes book of a seven-book series stood on its own. Its FB2, made with calibre for a SelfPub
edition, has no `<sequence>` at all, while the next volume's has one. Guessing the series from the title
was turned down in favour of letting the reader say: the book details page has a Series and volume
section whose editor offers the writer's other series, and the correction lives in `book_series_edit`.
The page's section headers were passed as plain strings and never localized; they're keys now.

## Which volume a book is

"99 мир" drew every book as volume 99. The titles share the figure, and the shelf trusted a figure read
off the titles over the one a file states whenever the titles told the books apart. Now a volume the
book states, in its FB2 or from the service, wins book by book, and the titles only number a book that
states none. Only a figure that changes from title to title counts as a volume, and a dash between the
name and its figure ("Name – 2. Subtitle") no longer leaves "2. Subtitle" behind.

"Дневной Дозор" states its series and volume but names its co-author first, and the shelf filed a series
under each book's first writer, so it stood apart from the other six. Books under one series name are
now one run when they share any writer.

## One field for a book's series

Merged series lived in `book_series` and a book's own correction in `book_series_edit`, and the merge
won on the way out, so a merged book's series couldn't be changed from its page. Both are now one row
per book in `book_series_override` (series, volume, and the place the reader dragged it to), and merging
writes the series name into it. Version 5 of the store folds the old merges in and drops `book_series`.
The editor is also on the shelf's book menu, as Series and volume.

## Plates and pills on the line

A series plate stood its box on the title's baseline, which beside a title a few sizes bigger puts it
well above the letters. Plates and pills now centre on the capitals of the text style they're given,
through `centredOnCapitals(of:)`, which replaces `sitsOnTheLine()`.

## Marking a book read

A book marked read changed from cover to spine in one frame, and the context menu's lifted copy of it
settled onto a shelf that had already moved. The list turned a card only when the whole card flipped;
it now turns one whenever any of its books changes stance, so the book turns on its hinge once the menu
has gone. A book that leaves its card, as one read through does under Reading, fades where it stood
while the rest close up. A simulator recording shows both, frame by frame.
