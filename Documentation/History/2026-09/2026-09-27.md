# 2026-09-27

## Control Center closed the book

Swiping down from the top edge to open Control Center closed the reader. The swipe is a downward
drag on the page, so the zoom back to the cover began; the system then took the touch, and UIKit's
zoom transition finishes a dismissal whose touch is cancelled rather than going back. There's no
public way to reverse one once it has begun.

So the dismissal never begins for a drag that started in the system's band. Each zoomed screen
carries a `TouchDownRecognizer` that records whether the latest touch came down above the window's top
safe-area inset and fails straight away, and `interactiveDismissShouldBegin` refuses the drag when it
did. A drag from anywhere lower still closes the book. The simulator can't make the system's edge
swipe, so this was checked on a device.
